USE RESPONSIBLY

Before you host, fork, or use a tool on a live estimate

01 / LICENSE

Open code ≠ free data

Follow each upstream license and retain required notices. Cost databases, drawings, templates, third-party dependencies, and trademarks may have separate restrictions.

02 / TAKEOFF

Calibrate and reconcile

Check sheet scales, units, overlaps, deductions, IFC classifications and quantities against source drawings. Automated results are not certification.

03 / ESTIMATING

Price for the right project

Rate libraries are not automatically current market quotations. Validate date, locale, labor productivity, tax, freight, waste and scope before pricing a bid.

04 / CONTRACTS

Preserve governing terms

Contract-control tools do not decide FIDIC entitlement. Engineer instructions, variations, time bars and payment procedures require the actual signed contract and supporting records.

Why some projects are not copied here

A construction cost dataset reviewed during research uses CC BY-NC 4.0 for data, and one Odoo contracting module has conflicting license signals (its README says OPL-1). Neither is mirrored or promoted here as unrestricted reusable code. A “free” web demo may also advertise a separately paid Excel product. Check upstream terms first.
