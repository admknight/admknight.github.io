USE RESPONSIBLY

Before you host, fork, or use a tool on a live estimate

01 / LICENSE

Personal use ≠ unlimited rights

Read the source license. Personal/internal-use grants may prohibit selling software; noncommercial cost datasets may not be used for paid estimating. Hosted apps may require payment even for private use.

02 / TAKEOFF

Calibrate and reconcile

Check sheet scales, units, overlaps, deductions, IFC classifications and quantities against source drawings. Automated results are not certification.

03 / ESTIMATING

Price for the right project

Rate libraries are not automatically current market quotations. Validate date, locale, labor productivity, tax, freight, waste and scope before pricing a bid.

04 / CONTRACTS

Preserve governing terms

Contract-control tools do not decide FIDIC entitlement. Engineer instructions, variations, time bars and payment procedures require the actual signed contract and supporting records.

Why some projects are not copied here

Previously omitted resources are now included as clearly labelled references. AutoConst takeoff skills have personal/internal-use licenses even where their READMEs say MIT. DDC cost data is CC BY-NC 4.0 with attribution. ConstructFlow lists LGPL-3 in files but OPL-1 in its README; verify permission with its author. Hosted MCP services and advertised paid plugins may require an account or purchase. We link to the original source only, and do not mirror restricted code or data.
